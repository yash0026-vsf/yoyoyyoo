# StatSkill AI

StatSkill AI is an AI-powered competency intelligence and personalized learning platform designed for India's government statistical workforce.

## Overview

The platform helps government employees:

- Assess their current competencies
- Identify skill gaps against role requirements
- Discover personalized learning paths
- Generate AI-powered quizzes from learning material
- Track learning and competency progress

Administrators can use workforce analytics to understand competency distributions, skill gaps, training progress, and emerging skill requirements.
